Conversion details

Pages fit proportionally, in their existing order and orientation. Ordinary PDFs retain text and graphics. PDFs with forms, signatures, annotations, or layers use a visual copy to include their rendered appearance. Visual copies contain page images at up to 300 dpi, reduced for very large pages. Text is no longer selectable, fields are not editable, and digital-signature validity is not retained.

Clickable links, bookmarks, attachments, accessibility tags, and popup comment text are not carried over. Check the preview and downloaded result, including visible fields and marks. Passwords are entered locally; the new copy is not password-protected. Damaged PDFs, printing restrictions, and dynamic XFA forms may require a standard PDF export from the original application. Limits: one PDF at a time, 150 MB, 1,000 pages, and 180 MB of converted page images.

Your PDF and password are not uploaded or stored by this tool. Only the download you choose to save creates a file. Clear PDF releases the document from the tool without deleting your original; closing the tab also clears the session. This is not a secure memory-erasure guarantee. Your browser, device, and download-folder sync follow their own policies.

Keep the tool close with your own shortcut.

Mac · desktop shortcut with our icon
  1. Drag this link onto your desktop: Page Fit.
  2. Download the Page Fit icon. Open it in Preview, press ⌘A, then ⌘C.
  3. Select the desktop shortcut and press ⌘I. Click the small icon at the top left of the Info window, then press ⌘V.

Already have a shortcut? Start with step 2.

Mac · add to Dock

In Safari on macOS Sonoma or later, choose File → Add to Dock, keep the name Page Fit, and click Add. The website supplies our logo for the app icon.

Windows · Chrome or Edge

If “Install Page Fit” appears above, select it. Otherwise, open your browser’s menu and look for Install Page Fit or Apps → Install this site as an app. Follow the browser’s options to create a desktop shortcut or pin it to your taskbar.

Your browser controls shortcut creation; this page cannot change desktop icons automatically. An internet connection may be needed to open the tool. Company device settings may limit installation.
